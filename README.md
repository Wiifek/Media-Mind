# MediaMind

**MediaMind is an AI-powered assistant that helps you understand audio and video content.**

Upload an MP3 or MP4 file, provide a URL, and let AI turn your media into actionable information. Get concise summaries, extract important items, and have a conversation with your content.

## ✨ Features

* 🎥 **Video & Audio Analysis** — Upload MP4, MP3, and other supported media files.
* 🔗 **URL Processing** — Provide a media URL and let the AI analyze its content.
* 📝 **AI Summaries** — Get concise summaries of long videos, podcasts, meetings, lectures, and more.
* 🔍 **Information Extraction** — Extract key points, action items, topics, names, dates, and other relevant information.
* 💬 **Chat With Your Media** — Ask questions about the content and get answers grounded in the uploaded media.
* 🧠 **AI-Powered Understanding** — Go beyond transcription by turning raw media into structured and useful information.

## 🚀 How It Works

```text
Upload MP3/MP4 or provide a URL
              ↓
       AI processes the media
              ↓
        Transcription & Analysis
              ↓
     ┌────────┼──────────┐
     ↓        ↓          ↓
  Summary  Extraction   Chat
```
